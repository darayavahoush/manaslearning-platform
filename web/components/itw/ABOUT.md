# About In Their World

Reading a diagnostic checklist about autism, ADHD, dyslexia, dyscalculia, or speech and communication differences tells you clinical terms — it does not always show how classroom environments and task demands shape a child's learning. **In Their World** provides interactive perspectives exploring environmental barriers, task design, and practical accommodations across neurodiversity.

These experiences are designed for parents and teachers to understand environmental arrangements, task friction, and evidence-based supports. They are educational tools, not diagnostic instruments, and never claim to simulate or recreate an individual's lived disability.

## The Modules

- **Autism** — explores how multi-sensory competition shapes attentional filtering in busy classrooms.
- **ADHD** — examines attentional regulation, working memory load, and environmental distractions during task execution.
- **Dyslexia** — explores the cognitive demands of phonological decoding and visual reader accommodations.
- **Speech & Communication (Communication Access Studio)** — explores classroom conversation partner practices for stuttering, AAC, and processing time.
- **Dyscalculia (The Quantity Kitchen)** — explores proportional reasoning through concrete manipulatives, ratio strips, and numeric notation.
- **Dysgraphia** — examines the ideation-to-transcription bottleneck and expressive assistive supports.
- **Auditory Processing** — explores signal-to-noise acoustic challenges in ambient classroom environments.
- **Executive Functioning (The Project Planning Desk)** — explores task initiation, Now/Next/Later visual horizons, workflow dependencies, and chunking.

Each module follows a structured pedagogical format:

1. **Explore Task Demands** — an interactive activity examining environmental barriers and immediate supports.
2. **See the Data** — carefully cited Indian epidemiological and clinical context with clear denominators.
3. **Know What Helps** — concrete, evidence-based accommodations for home, school, and examinations.
