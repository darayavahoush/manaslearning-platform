# Play-Based Assessment Quest Assets Manifest

All illustrations and sprites are original, child-safe, royalty-free storybook gouache artworks inspired by Indian settings (courtyards, neem and gulmohar trees, night jasmine, carved balustrades, brass lanterns).

## Art Style Bible
*"soft hand-painted storybook illustration, warm gouache texture, rounded friendly shapes, gentle colour palette, calm and uncluttered, high contrast between subject and background, no text in image, no harsh shadows, child-safe, inclusive and Indian-inspired setting"*
