## Audio Specifications
- **Format**: Mono MP3, 64 kbps, 44,100 Hz.
- **Normalization**: Sensory-safe gentle master headroom (-3 dB peak, soft decay envelopes).
- **Sensory Safety**: No harsh buzzers, sudden percussive transients, or volume spikes. Neutral, non-punishing feedback for error states.
